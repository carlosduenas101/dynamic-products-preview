The company at a glance

Two plants, one mile apart, making fittings since 1969

Founded by Ron Lindquist and Roger Black, Dynamic Products is privately held and has operated in Houston since 1970. About 80 people work at the main plant in sales, administration, and production: receiving, raw material storage, cutting, forming, bending, mandrel forming, heat treatment, sand blasting, quality lab, final inspection, and shipping.

About 10 more work at the second location, a machining center for beveling with additional raw material storage.

Milestones

  1. 1969Founded in Oregon to serve paper mill piping, supplying the Gulf Coast.
  2. 1970Moved to Houston to be closer to Gulf Coast customers.
  3. 1972ASME U and PP certification.
  4. 1984Pipe bending machine with capacity up to 60" diameter.
  5. 1985Butt-weld production expanded to 92". Sales reps added on East and West Coasts.
  6. 1986Bought the Houston plant for bending and butt-weld production. Mandrel forming up to 48".
  7. 1995Second Houston facility opened as the machining center.
  8. 1998Canadian Registration Numbers (CSA) obtained.
  9. 2008PED certification.
