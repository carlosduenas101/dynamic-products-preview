Specifying a fitting

Four details get you a quote

  1. 01Fitting typeElbow, bend, tee, reducer, cap, cross, lateral or wye, with the angle or radius.
  2. 02Size and wallOutside diameter up to 92" and wall thickness up to 4", with end sizes for reducers and reducing tees.
  3. 03Material and gradeOne of seven material families, with the specification and grade, such as ASTM A234 WPB.
  4. 04Standards and testsASME B16.9, MSS SP-75, NACE, PED or your own specification, with any special testing.
